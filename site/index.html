Manifesto

Your machine is enough.

Your projects already live on your Mac — the tool that manages them should too. Foldview scans, launches, and tiles with zero runtime dependencies: no daemon, no account, no telemetry.

The network is opt-in. A push, a model catalog probe, a page you chose to open — nothing happens in the background on your behalf.

Quiet tools last. One file, one terminal, every project in orbit.
